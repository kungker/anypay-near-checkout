How settlement works

1. AnyPay asks NEAR Intents for a quote: your asset on your chain → USDC on NEAR.
2. In production, the API returns a one-time deposit address. You send your asset there — no wallet connection, no signing inside AnyPay.
3. Solvers compete to fill the intent; the merchant's NEAR account receives USDC, typically in under a minute.
4. If anything goes wrong before the deadline, the deposit is refunded to your refund address.
